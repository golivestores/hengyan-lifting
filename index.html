About Hengyan

An Industry Veteran in
Light Hoisting Equipment

Hebei Hengyan Hoisting Machinery Co., Ltd. has been adhering to the philosophy of "Technology as the Core, Quality as the Foundation, Service as the Support" since its establishment.

We specialize in R&D and manufacturing of electric hoists, chain blocks, intelligent lifting platforms, customized micro-motors, and high-precision winches — providing integrated "Product + Solution + O&M" services for industrial production, construction, and agricultural operations.

Our products have covered 34 provincial-level regions across China, and entered infrastructure projects in 20+ countries across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Africa through the Belt & Road network.

Quality Assurance

Three-Level Inspection System

Step 01 — Incoming

Raw Material Verification

Every raw material batch undergoes 12-index verification including spectral analysis and hardness testing before warehouse entry.

Step 02 — Production

Full-Process Traceability

Each unit receives a unique quality QR code for real-time access to CNC cutting, high-precision welding, and painting process parameters.

Step 03 — Testing

Simulated Working Conditions

Finished products undergo extreme overload and continuous start-stop tests on our simulation platform — ensuring 100% qualification rate.
