Welcome To Our Company

An Enterprise Specializing In The Manufacturing Of Light And Small Lifting Equipment

Hebei Hengyan Hoisting Machinery Co., Ltd., located in Qingyuan County, Baoding City, Hebei Province, is a specialized enterprise focused on the R&D, manufacturing, and sales of lifting equipment. The company offers a diverse range of products, including electric hoists, chain blocks, winches, intelligent lifting platforms, customized micro-motors, and high-precision winches — providing integrated "Product + Solution + O&M" services for industrial production, construction, and agricultural operations.
