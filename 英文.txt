Hebei Hengyan Hoisting Machinery Co., Ltd., as an industry veteran deeply rooted in China's light and small hoisting equipment sector, has been adhering to the development philosophy of "Technology as the Core, Quality as the Foundation, and Service as the Support" since its establishment. We specialize in the R&D and manufacturing of light and small hoisting equipment, core components of hoisting machinery, intelligent lifting platforms, customized micro-motors, and high-precision winches. Meanwhile, we offer a full-chain service package covering mechanical equipment integration and supporting, specialized hardware tool supply, electromechanical equipment system debugging, scenario-based adaptation of construction machinery, customized development of basket motors, and installation of hoisting modules for agricultural machinery. We provide integrated "Product + Solution + Operation & Maintenance" services for customers in industrial production, construction, agricultural operations and other fields.

In the production and quality control processes, the company has invested heavily in introducing modern production lines such as CNC cutting, high-precision welding, and intelligent painting. A three-level quality inspection system covering "raw material incoming inspection - full-process production node traceability - finished product simulated working condition testing" has been established. Raw materials can only be warehoused after passing 12-index verification including spectral analysis and hardness testing. Each piece of equipment is assigned a unique "quality QR code" during production, allowing real-time access to process parameters. Finished products are subjected to extreme tests such as overload and continuous start-stop on a test platform simulating actual working scenarios, ultimately ensuring a 100% qualified rate for ex-factory products.

Our products have covered 34 provincial-level administrative regions across China, and entered industrial and infrastructure projects in more than 20 countries and regions in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa and other areas through the agent network along the "Belt and Road" Initiative. We have served over 12,000 customers in total, and earned a solid reputation for "durable equipment", "fast response speed" and "customized solutions tailored to needs" in scenarios such as port loading and unloading, factory relocation, and agricultural greenhouse construction.
